## 1. Source and scope

The selected open educational resource is MIT OpenCourseWare's *6.036 Introduction to Machine Learning (Fall 2020)*:

- Course page: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-036-introduction-to-machine-learning-fall-2020/
- Lecture 1 slides: https://people.csail.mit.edu/tbroderick/files/ml_6036_2020_lectures/broderick_lecture_01.pdf

MIT describes the course as covering machine-learning principles and applications from the viewpoint of modelling and prediction, including representation, over-fitting, generalization, supervised learning and reinforcement learning.

**Scope note:** This is an initial concept-level comparison of the website's Machine Learning notes with the course description and introductory lecture slides. It is not a line-by-line verification of every sentence. The email-spam example below is an added teaching example, not claimed to be a quote or example from MIT.

## 3. What changed for accessibility?

- Long or technical ideas were shortened into familiar words and short sentences.
- Brief definitions were added beside terms such as supervised learning, overfitting and generalization.
- A familiar spam-email example was added to make the topic concrete.
- A short audio script and a visual step summary were prepared for learners who prefer listening or visual cues.

## 4. Information that may be reduced or lost

- The simplified version omits mathematical notation, assumptions and technical details from a university-level course.
- The reinforcement-learning explanation is introductory and does not cover all of the formal components.
- The website's short “collect data → train model → test model → predict” flow is a teaching summary, not a universal sequence for every machine-learning workflow.
- The website has short notes on this topic; these notes should not be presented as a replacement for the complete course.
