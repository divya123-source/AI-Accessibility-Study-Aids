# AI Accessibility Study Aids — Prompt Library

**Project:** CAPSTONE 14 — AI Accessibility Study Aids  
**Purpose:** Reusable prompts for simplifying study material and producing accessible learning formats.

> **Use responsibly:** Add the exact source text and citation where indicated. Check AI outputs against the original material. These prompts do not replace source verification or human review. Do not invent peer feedback or tool-comparison results.

## Prompt 1 — Simplify study material

You are helping make study material accessible to learners who prefer plain English. Rewrite the source text below in clear, beginner-friendly English.

Requirements:
- Preserve the original meaning and important details.
- Explain technical words briefly.
- Keep key terms and definitions accurate.
- Use short sentences, headings, and a simple example if the source supports one.
- Do not add unsupported facts. If something is unclear, mark it as needing verification.
- After the rewrite, list key ideas that must be checked against the source.

**Source title and location:** [insert title, section, URL/page]  
**Original text:** [paste a short excerpt you are allowed to use]

## Prompt 2 — Make a short audio script

Turn the verified simplified text below into a natural spoken script of about 30–60 seconds.

Requirements:
- Use clear, easy-to-pronounce English.
- Keep all essential ideas and important caveats.
- Avoid adding facts that are not in the source.
- Write words in full rather than relying on abbreviations.
- Output only the script, then provide an approximate speaking time.

**Verified simplified text:** [paste text]

## Prompt 3 — Create a visual summary

Create a concise text-based visual-summary plan for the verified study material below. It will be made into a mind map or infographic for students.

Include:
- One central topic.
- Three to five main branches.
- Short labels and simple relationships/arrows.
- A small example only if supported by the source.
- A note of any concept that needs human verification.

Do not invent or change the meaning of the source.

**Verified study material:** [paste text]

## Prompt 4 — Verify simplified text against the original

Act as a careful reviewer. Compare the original source excerpt with the simplified version. Do not assume the simplified version is correct.

Create a table with these columns:
1. Concept or claim
2. What the original says
3. What the simplified version says
4. Status: Supported / Partly supported / Missing / Possibly distorted / Not enough evidence
5. Correction needed
6. Source location to re-check

Check definitions, limitations, examples, and whether important meaning was omitted. Quote only short excerpts. If the supplied source does not support a claim, say “not verified from the supplied material.”

**Simplified version:** [paste]

## Prompt 5 — Compare three AI tools fairly

I am comparing outputs from three AI tools for an accessibility study-aid task. Apply the same task and source text to each tool. Do not claim that you ran another tool or saw its output.

Give me a comparison rubric with scores from 1–5 for:
- Accuracy against the source
- Preservation of key ideas
- Plain-language clarity
- Accessibility and readability
- Unsupported additions or omissions

Define what a low and high score mean. Leave the actual scores blank until the real outputs have been reviewed. Then suggest how to record strengths, weaknesses, and corrections.

## Prompt 6 — Accessibility and tone review

Review the learning material below for accessibility and respectful tone.

Check for:
- Long or complicated sentences
- Unexplained technical vocabulary
- Ambiguous instructions
- Overly childish, patronising, or insensitive wording
- Formatting that may be hard to read with a screen reader
- Missing context or caveats

Suggest minimal edits and explain why. Do not change technical meaning. Flag anything that requires subject-matter review.

**Material to review:** [paste text]

Use neutral wording and make it possible to answer honestly, including negative feedback. Do not fill in answers or create fictional results.

## Prompt 9 — Draft a one-page repeatable workflow guide

Write a one-page guide for repeating this process with another educational chapter:
1. Select and cite an open educational source.
2. Choose a short section and preserve the original.
3. Generate a plain-language version.
4. Create a short audio script and a visual summary.
5. Compare all formats against the source.
6. Have a human check accuracy and tone.
7. Ask three real peers for feedback and record their actual responses.
8. Compare outputs from three AI tools using the same prompt and rubric.
9. Document revisions, limitations, and final files.

Use practical language. Clearly distinguish completed work from planned work. Include accuracy, tone, privacy, and human-review limitations.
